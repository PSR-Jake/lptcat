About LPT-Cat

LPT-Cat is a curated catalog of Long-Period Transients (LPTs), a recently emerging population of radio sources characterized by minute- to hour-long periodicities. These objects probe a largely unexplored region of neutron-star and stellar-transient parameter space.

This catalog compiles published and candidate LPTs from the literature, including their positions, periods, spin-down constraints, dispersion measures (DM), rotation measures (RM), and key observational notes. Upper limits and measurement uncertainties are presented following standard conventions used in astronomical catalogs.

If you use this catalog in your research, please cite the relevant discovery papers listed in the Reference column.

DM and RM uncertainties are shown as ±1σ. Upper limits on Ṗ are indicated when available.
